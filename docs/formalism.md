# Temporal Quantum Graph Neural Network on the E47 carrier

This note defines the network, states what is proved, and points to the check
in `validation/tqgnn_validation.py` that executes each statement. Evidence
classes follow the E47 convention: **E0** exact proof, **E1** executable
reconstruction, **E2** simulation, **OPEN** unresolved (see `scope.md`).

## 0. Ingredients

**Time formalism.** From *Time, Protected Memory, and E47: A Prima Facie
Review*: the Casimir-spectral pullback metric, the time-chain identity, the
sector-phase clock, the matched monitored recovery, and the prime clock. The
canonical runner `validation/e47_spacetime_prima_facie.py` is included
unchanged (26/26).

**QGNN formalism.** Verdon et al., *Quantum Graph Neural Networks*
(arXiv:1909.12264): a parametrised evolution generated by graph-structured
Hamiltonians

$$
\hat H(\theta)=\sum_{(j,k)\in E}\sum_r W^{(r)}_{jk}\,\hat O^{(r)}_j\otimes\hat P^{(r)}_k+\sum_{v\in V}\sum_r B^{(r)}_v\,\hat R^{(r)}_v ,
$$

whose recurrent form (QGRNN) is learned from time series of local observables.

**The integration.** Every node of the graph is an E47 clock-memory unit that
moves on its own worldline. Graph Hamiltonians and recovery channels run in
each node's proper time, and the network is executed in two ways: as a
click-resolved stochastic process, and as the deterministic lapse-scaled
GKSL flow. A single parameter, the detection efficiency $\eta$, moves the same
network continuously from the first to the second.

## 1. The carrier is a graph

Sites $n=25a+5b+c$, $a,b,c\in\{0,\dots,4\}$, carry $m=(2-a,2-b,2-c)$. The
total Casimir $C=\mathbf J_{\rm tot}^2$ is a real symmetric operator on the
125 sites.

- **(E0/E1, A3)** $C_{uv}\neq0$ for $u\neq v$ only when $m(u)-m(v)=e_a-e_b$
  ($a\neq b$): $C=\sum_a\mathbf J_a^2+2\sum_{a<b}\mathbf J_a\cdot\mathbf J_b$,
  and $\mathbf J_a\cdot\mathbf J_b$ moves one unit up on axis $a$ and one down
  on axis $b$. The graph $G_C$ is a union of 13 planar patches
  $m_1+m_2+m_3=M$ of sizes $1,3,6,10,15,18,19,18,15,10,6,3,1$ (A4).
- **(E0)** $E_{47}=\ker K$, $K=(C-6I)(C-30I)$, is the spectral band
  $\{6,30\}$ of $G_C$ (A1, A2).
- **(E0, A5)** $\Gamma=I-K^2/99144$ is a degree-4 polynomial in $C$, so
  $\Gamma_{uv}=0$ whenever $d_{G_C}(u,v)>4$: one application is a 4-hop
  message-passing step.
- **(E0, A6)** $\|\Gamma^n-P\|_2=(15/17)^n$. The E47 projector is the limit
  of a deep linear spectral graph-convolutional network with fixed filter
  $\Gamma$.

With $S=\mathbf J_1\cdot\mathbf J_2+\mathbf J_2\cdot\mathbf J_3+\mathbf J_3\cdot\mathbf J_1$
and $C=3j(j+1)+2S$,

$$
K_j=\bigl(C-j(j+1)\bigr)\bigl(C-5j(j+1)\bigr)=4\bigl(S^2-[j(j+1)]^2\bigr),
\qquad K_2=(C-6I)(C-30I),
$$

so E47 keeps the states whose total pairwise correlation is $\pm j(j+1)$.

## 2. Time: metric, lapse, time-chain identity

- **(E1, B1)** $g=B_4^\dagger(P_6-P_0)B_4={\rm diag}(-1,2,8,2)$ (reconstructed
  in `tqgnn/spacetime.py`; the canonical runner checks the rest of the chain).
- **Lapse (B2).** For a worldline $x(t)=(t,\mathbf x(t))$ with velocity
  $\mathbf v$,
  $N=d\tau/dt=\sqrt{-g(\dot x,\dot x)}=\sqrt{1-2v_1^2-8v_2^2-2v_3^2}$,
  defined only for timelike worldlines.
- **Time-chain identity (E0, B3, D3).** If $d\rho/d\tau=\mathcal L_\tau\rho$
  with $\mathcal L_\tau\rho=-i[H_\tau,\rho]+\sum_\alpha\mathcal D[R_\alpha]\rho$,
  then $\rho(t):=\rho_\tau(\tau(t))$ satisfies

  $$
  \frac{d\rho}{dt}=N(t)\,\mathcal L_\tau\rho,\qquad H_t=NH_\tau,\qquad R_{\alpha,t}=\sqrt N\,R_{\alpha,\tau},
  $$

  because $\mathcal D[\sqrt N R]=N\,\mathcal D[R]$.
- **Preservation (E1, B4).** The frame's time direction $u$ is the Casimir
  singlet: $K^2u=32400\,u$, $\langle u|\Gamma|u\rangle=0.6732026144$. The
  geometry is background structure; the network filters states, never the
  frame.

## 3. Node: the E47 clock-memory unit

**States.** $|a\rangle$ = pair singlet of spins 1, 2 $\otimes|m_3=0\rangle$
($C=6$, the canonical runner's copy); $|b\rangle$, $|a'\rangle$, $|b'\rangle$ =
normalised projections of the site $m=(1,0,-1)$ onto $C=30$, $C=12$, $C=42$.
$V=[a,b]$ lies in E47, $W=[a',b']$ outside it (C1).

**Generator (proper time).** $f$ is the clock; the review's linear clock is
$f(C)=\omega C/24$, so $f(30)-f(6)=\omega$.

$$
H=f(C)+c\,(WV^\dagger+VW^\dagger),\qquad R=\sqrt\gamma\,VW^\dagger .
$$

- **Exact reduction (E0, C2).** $H$, $R$, $R^\dagger R$ map
  ${\rm span}\{V,W\}$ into itself, so the 125-dimensional dynamics of any state
  in that span is the 4-dimensional dynamics on logical $\otimes$ flag
  (flag 0 = in E47). Retention is ${\rm Tr}(P\rho)$ = flag-0 population.
- **Clock (E0, C3).** With $c=0$ and $\rho(0)=|{+}\rangle\langle{+}|$:
  $\langle |a\rangle\langle b|+|b\rangle\langle a|\rangle=\cos\omega\tau$ and
  ${\rm Tr}(P\rho)=1$.
- **Leak and recovery (E0, C4, C5).** Each branch $l\in\{a,b\}$ is a driven,
  damped two-level system with detuning $\Delta_l=f(C_{l'})-f(C_l)$:

  $$
  q_l=\frac{4c^2}{\gamma^2+8c^2+4\Delta_l^2},\qquad \lambda_l=\gamma\,q_l .
  $$

  At $\Delta=0$, $c=1$: $\gamma=20\Rightarrow$ retention 99.019608 %, clicks
  0.19607843; $\gamma=40\Rightarrow$ 99.751244 %, 0.09950249.
- **Click statistics (E1, C6).** Recovery clicks are sub-Poissonian; the
  asymptotic Fano factor at $\Delta=0$ is
  $F=1-24c^2\gamma^2/(\gamma^2+8c^2)^2$ (the resonance-fluorescence value with
  Rabi frequency $2c$), from full counting statistics of the tilted generator.

### 3.1 A running clock and logical-blind recovery

The review's recovery preserves logical information. With the clock running,
that holds only under a condition on the spectrum.

- **(E0, A7)** Among the Casimir eigenvalues $\{0,2,6,12,20,30,42\}$, only
  $(6,30)$ differ by 24.
- **(E0, C8)** Write $f(C_{l'})=f(C_l)+\Delta_l$. If $\Delta_a=\Delta_b$, then
  $H=H_L\otimes I+I\otimes H_F$ and $R=I\otimes\sqrt\gamma\sigma^-$, so the
  logical clock evolves unitarily whatever the flag does.
- **Consequence.** For the linear clock, $\Delta_a=\Delta_b$ requires two
  sectors outside E47 that are 24 apart, and none exist. The branches leak
  with different detunings, recovery clicks carry which-sector information, and
  the clock dephases. To second order in $c/\gamma$ (E1, C7), with
  $z_l=(\Delta_l-i\gamma/2)^{-1}$:

  $$
  \frac{d\rho_{ab}}{dt}=\Bigl[i\omega+ic^2\bigl(z_a-\bar z_b\bigr)+\gamma c^2 z_a\bar z_b\Bigr]\rho_{ab},
  \qquad
  \gamma_\varphi=\tfrac12\gamma c^2\,|z_a-z_b|^2 .
  $$

- **Remedy (E0, C8).** A spectral clock $f$ equal to $\omega C/24$ on every
  sector except one syndrome sector, moved so that $\Delta_a=\Delta_b$,
  preserves the clock exactly under leak and recovery. Inside E47 it is the
  same clock.

## 4. The network

**Definition.** Graph $G=(V,E)$; node $v$ carries the reduced unit (dimension
4) and a worldline with lapse $N_v(t)$. The proper-time generator is
graph-structured in the QGNN sense:

$$
H_\tau=\sum_{v}H_v+\sum_{(j,k)\in E}\Bigl[J_{jk}\bigl(s^+_js^-_k+s^-_js^+_k\bigr)+Z_{jk}\,z_jz_k\Bigr],
\qquad\{R_v\}_{v\in V},
$$

with $s^+=|b\rangle\langle a|\otimes I_F$ and $z=(|a\rangle\langle a|-|b\rangle\langle b|)\otimes I_F$.
In coordinate time every term runs on its own worldline:

$$
H_t=\sum_v N_v(t)H_v+\sum_e N_e(t)H_e,\qquad R_{v,t}=\sqrt{N_v(t)}\,R_v,\qquad N_e=\tfrac12(N_j+N_k).
$$

A layer is the CPTP map over one coordinate-time step; stacked layers are the
recurrent QGNN unrolled in time. Readouts per node: retention
${\rm Tr}(P_v\rho)$, clock coherence ${\rm Tr}(|a\rangle\langle b|_v\rho)$,
click intensity $N_v\gamma\,{\rm Tr}((1-P_v)\rho)$.

- **(E0, D2)** For $N_v,N_e\ge0$ the generator is GKSL at every $t$, so each
  layer is CPTP.
- **(E0, D3)** Equal lapses reduce the network to the time-chain identity.
- **(E0, D1)** Without edges or leakage, node $v$'s clock phase is
  $\omega\tau_v(t)$: each clock reads its own proper time.
- **(E0, D4)** Message passing between clocks on different worldlines is
  detuned. In the one-excitation pair $\{|ba\rangle,|ab\rangle\}$ the detuning
  is $\delta=(N_j-N_k)\omega$ and the coupling $N_eJ$, so the maximal transfer
  is $4N_e^2J^2/(4N_e^2J^2+\delta^2)$.

**Learning (E0/E2, L1–L3).** Coordinate-time data depend on the parameters
only through $N_v\omega_v$ and $N_eJ_e$. Lapses are therefore not identifiable
from uncalibrated clocks: the Jacobian has one flat direction per node. With
identical calibrated clocks ($\omega_v=\omega$ known), QGRNN-style training
recovers every lapse. Training starts from each clock's early phase slope and
fits on growing time windows. On noiseless data it returns the metric lapses
$1,\ \sqrt{0.82},\ \sqrt{0.74}$ to machine precision.

## 5. From stochastic to dynamic

**Unravelling with efficiency $\eta$.** Detect each recovery click with
probability $\eta$. Between detected clicks the unnormalised conditional state
obeys the linear equation below, and $\Pr(\text{no detected click})={\rm Tr}\,\tilde\rho$.

$$
\frac{d\tilde\rho}{dt}=\mathcal L_t\tilde\rho-\eta\sum_v J_v\tilde\rho,
\qquad J_v\rho=R_{v,t}\rho R_{v,t}^\dagger .
$$

At a detected click in channel $v$, $\rho\to J_v\rho/{\rm Tr}(J_v\rho)$.

- **Invariance (E0, S2, S5).** $\mathbb E[\rho_c(t)]=\rho(t)$ for every
  $\eta$. The drift and the compensator cancel in expectation.
- **Dynamic limit (E0, S1).** At $\eta=0$ nothing is recorded and
  $\rho_c(t)=\rho(t)$: the system is the deterministic lapse-scaled flow.
- **Monotone transition (E0, S4).** A record at $\eta'<\eta$ is an independent
  thinning of the record at $\eta$. Hence
  $\rho_c^{\eta'}=\mathbb E[\rho_c^{\eta}\mid\text{record}_{\eta'}]$, and for
  every observable the conditional variance is non-decreasing in $\eta$ and
  zero at $\eta=0$.
- **Click statistics (E0, S3, S6).** The detected rate is $\eta\lambda$ and the
  Fano factor is $F_\eta=1+\eta(F-1)$ (independent thinning).
- **Click time (E2, S7).** Counting clicks estimates proper time with relative
  error $\sqrt{F_\eta/(\eta\lambda\tau)}$. As $\eta\to0$ the click clock fails,
  and time is read dynamically from the clock phase instead.
- **Zeno route (E1, S8).** Raising $\gamma$ is a second route. The click rate
  $4c^2\gamma/(\gamma^2+8c^2)\to4c^2/\gamma$, and the clock dephasing
  $\gamma_\varphi\simeq8c^2(\Delta_a-\Delta_b)^2/\gamma^3$, so the protected
  clock becomes deterministic.

**Operational summary.** Each notion of time has its own observable:

| notion | observable | in the network |
|---|---|---|
| proper time $\tau_v$ | lapse along the worldline | $N_v(t)$ from $g$ |
| clock phase | ${\rm Tr}(\lvert a\rangle\langle b\rvert_v\rho)$ | reads $\omega\tau_v$ (D1) |
| click time | detected recovery count | precision $\sqrt{F_\eta/(\eta\lambda\tau)}$ (S7) |
| prime cycle | $U_{47}$ on E47, $U_{47}\otimes U_5$ | periods 47 and 235 (P1, P2); seconds uncalibrated |
